About the School

As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa rahmatullāhi wa barakātuh

Al-Forqan Quran School was established with the mission of making quality Quranic education accessible to every Muslim in our community. Our dedicated teachers guide students from the very first letters of the Arabic alphabet through to the completion of Hifz (full memorisation of the Quran).

Enrollment is open throughout the year. Fill in the registration form and our admissions team will contact you within 2 working days to confirm your place and class assignment.
